================================================================
  BtP OVEN  -  LICENSING IN PLAIN ENGLISH
================================================================

This page answers the two questions that actually matter:

  A. Can I sell music I make with BtP Oven?
  B. Can I sell or give away the plugin itself?

They have different answers. Read the one you need.

This is a practical summary, not legal advice. If money gets serious,
have a lawyer read THIRD-PARTY.txt.


================================================================
A. SELLING MUSIC MADE WITH THE PLUGIN
================================================================

Short answer: YES for normal beat-making, with one exception
(other people's voices). RAVE output is covered separately below.

  FEATURE                        SELL THE RESULT?
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  ACE-Step instrumental           YES
  ACE-Step vocals / hooks         YES
  ACE-Step one-shots              YES
  Demucs stem separation          YES
  RVC voice conversion            YES, if the voice is yours or
                                  you have permission
  Auto-tune / FX / mastering      YES
  RAVE MUTATE variants            YES, per the model authors -
                                  see the note below

RAVE: what the authors actually say
  The RAVE framework and the voice_multi weights are CC-BY-NC 4.0.
  The Intelligent Instruments Lab, who trained voice_multi, describe
  the limit in their own release notes:

    "All our RAVE models inherit the Creative Commons Noncommercial
     license required by the ACIDS software, meaning you can do just
     about anything with them except sell the models themselves as
     part of a product."

  That draws the line at the models, not at music made with them.
  So selling a beat that contains a RAVE variant is consistent with
  the authors' stated position - what you may not do is ship the
  models inside a product you sell.

  Practical rule used by this project:
    * making and selling music with RAVE ............ fine
    * shipping RAVE models in a paid plugin ......... not fine
    * shipping RAVE models in a free plugin ......... fine, with
                                                     attribution

  If you want zero ambiguity, email the lab and keep the reply:
    thormagnusson@hi.is
    https://huggingface.co/Intelligent-Instruments-Lab/rave-models

  Credit them whenever you use the models:
    Intelligent Instruments Lab. rave-models. 2023.
    https://huggingface.co/Intelligent-Instruments-Lab/rave-models
    doi:10.57967/hf/1235

What about the AI VOCALS specifically?
  * The audio itself is yours to use. RVC and ACE-Step do not claim
    a royalty on your output.
  * The voice matters more than the tool. Training on your own voice,
    a hired singer, or a voice you have written permission to use is
    fine. Cloning a famous artist, or a singer who did not agree, is
    not - even for a free beat.
  * Keep a note of consent. A one-line message from a singer saying
    "you can use my voice for these tracks" is worth keeping.

Copyright and disclosure
  * In the US and several other countries, purely AI-generated audio
    may not be copyrightable on its own, because copyright needs
    human authorship. Your arrangement, sound design, editing and
    mixing are human authorship - keep the project files as evidence.
  * Major platforms (Spotify, YouTube, TikTok) increasingly require
    AI disclosure. Label AI vocals where the platform asks. It costs
    nothing and avoids takedowns.

Lyrics
  * Do not paste lyrics you do not own into the LYRICS box.
  * Generated lyrics can occasionally echo training data. Read them
    before release; you are responsible for what you ship.


================================================================
ROYALTY-FREE LOOPS AND SAMPLES
================================================================

Your workflow - chop it, move the notes, process it until the source
is unrecognisable - is normal production. It does not change the
licence, though.

  * The loop pack licence still applies to the recording.
  * Most royalty-free licences allow commercial songs and forbid
    redistributing the loop "as a loop" or inside another sample pack.
  * "You can't hear the original anymore" is not a legal defence,
    but in practice most licences already permit what you are doing.
  * Keep the receipt / licence text for each pack. A folder called
    Licences\ with the PDF or screenshot is enough.
  * Do not put a commercial loop into Training\ and then share the
    trained model unless the pack licence allows training.


================================================================
B. SELLING OR SHARING THE PLUGIN
================================================================

AS PACKAGED TODAY: two builds, two rules.

  PERSONAL / FREE BUILD     RAVE included, free to use and share
  COMMERCIAL BUILD          RAVE removed, may be sold
                            (-DBTP_ENABLE_RAVE=OFF, -NoRave)

Two things decide which is which:

  1. RAVE (code + voice_multi weights) is CC-BY-NC 4.0. That is the
     only blocker for selling, and it is a build-time switch.

  2. JUCE 9 is licensed under AGPLv3 or a JUCE tier. The free tier
     (Starter) covers revenue up to $20,000/year; Indie ($800
     perpetual) covers up to $300k; Pro ($3,500 perpetual) is
     unlimited. If you distribute under AGPLv3 instead, you must
     publish the complete corresponding source.

     Indie is a commercial licence: it permits selling closed-source
     binaries up to $300k/year. A monthly subscription and the
     perpetual buy give the same rights - the perpetual just stops
     the monthly payment.

WHAT YOU CAN DO RIGHT NOW
  * Use it yourself, for free, including on music you sell.
  * Give the personal build to friends for free.
  * Publish the personal build for free with this file,
    THIRD-PARTY.txt and the LICENSES folder.
  * Sell a commercial build (no RAVE) under an Indie licence.

WHAT YOU CANNOT DO AS PACKAGED
  * Sell the personal build while it contains the CC-BY-NC RAVE
    engine.
  * Ship RAVE models inside any paid product.
  * Relicense it as closed source without a JUCE licence.

TO MAKE A SELLABLE BUILD
  1. Keep your JUCE licence active. Indie covers up to $300k.
  2. Configure with -DBTP_ENABLE_RAVE=OFF and package with -NoRave.
  3. Keep every MIT / Apache / MPL notice and the LICENSES folder.
  4. Keep the ACE-Step NOTICE and state any files you modified.
  5. Re-audit THIRD-PARTY.txt. Anything marked NC must be gone -
     or, if the NO-RAVE.txt marker is present, confirm the entry is
     marked as not applicable.

Step-by-step build commands are in BUILD-VARIANTS.txt.

The rest of the stack - ACE-Step (Apache-2.0 code, MIT weights),
ggml, Demucs, Eigen, ONNX Runtime, RVC, ContentVec, RMVPE - is
compatible with a commercial product as long as notices are kept.

NOTE ON THE JUCE REVENUE LIMIT
  It is based on your previous 12 months and, for an individual,
  covers revenue from your use of the Framework from all sources.
  Beat sales and plugin sales both count. Under $20k, Starter is
  enough; between $20k and $300k, Indie covers you; above that,
  Pro. If the boundary matters, ask sales@juce.com - they will not
  give legal advice, but they can confirm how the tier applies.

DO YOU NEED TO BUY NOW?
  If you already run Indie, you are covered for selling. The
  perpetual buy is a cash-flow decision, not a permission one.
  Do not cancel the subscription until the perpetual licence is
  issued, or you will have a gap while still distributing builds.


================================================================
TRAINING DATA - THE PART PEOPLE FORGET
================================================================

  * Train only on audio you recorded, own, or that is explicitly
    royalty-free AND allows model training.
  * Do not scrape an artist's catalogue to build a voice.
  * Do not train on a purchased loop pack unless its licence says
    training is allowed.
  * If you publish a trained model, it carries the rights of its
    training data, not just the code licence.
  * Keep a simple log: source, date, permission. Future you will
    thank present you.


================================================================
GOOD HABITS
================================================================

  1. Keep the LICENSES\ folder with the app. It is installed to
     %APPDATA%\BtP Oven\Legal\.
  2. Add one row to THIRD-PARTY.txt whenever you add a dependency,
     at the moment you add it.
  3. Save the licence for every sample pack and voice you use.
  4. When unsure, prefer MIT / Apache-2.0 / BSD for code and
     MIT / Apache-2.0 for weights. Avoid NC and "research only"
     if you plan to sell.
  5. Attribution is a courtesy; the licence is the permission.
     Do both.
